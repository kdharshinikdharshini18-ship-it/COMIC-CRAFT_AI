The Fallen Star of Beacon Rock
==============================

PANEL 1
Comic book art style. High-angle shot of Mara Vale, a rugged lighthouse keeper in a bright yellow slicker, gripping the railing of an ancient stone lighthouse catwalk. A violent tempest rages with massive sea foam waves crashing below, while a radiant, golden-streaked celestial star plummets from the storm clouds into the churning dark ocean, casting dramatic lens flares and neon blue light across the rain-swept scene.

PANEL 2
Comic book art style. Mara Vale rushing down a spiral iron staircase inside the lighthouse, a brass lantern swinging in her hand. Her face is determined and alert, head tilted as glowing, runic soundwave vibrations emanate visually through the stone walls, showing the mystical, crystalline whisper calling to her from the deep sea.

PANEL 3
Comic book art style. Wide dramatic shot of Mara standing on the jagged, wet rocks of the storm-battered island shore. Lightning splits the purple-black sky behind her. A brilliant underwater glow illuminates the turbulent surf, revealing the submerged star pulsing like a radiant heartbeat beneath the crashing waves.

PANEL 4
Comic book art style. Dynamic action angle. Mara Vale fearlessly leaps from a slippery sea boulder onto a submerged ledge, braving the surging tide. She extends a brass-hooked rescue pole and reaches her hand into the frothing, luminescent water, wind whipping her dark hair across her face.

PANEL 5
Comic book art style. Close-up, awe-inspiring climactic shot. From beneath the swirling surface of the glowing sea, a celestial, crystalline hand made of starlight reaches up to touch Mara's outstretched fingers. Golden cosmic light illuminates Mara's wide, adventurous eyes as the storm clears into starlight above.
